# Hull Breach rules

**Setting:** a damaged space station. The loyal **crew** must complete repair missions. Hidden **saboteurs** among them want the missions to fail.

## Setup
- 5–10 players. Saboteurs: 2 with 5–6 players, 3 with 7–9, 4 with 10. Everyone else is loyal crew.
- Roles are dealt secretly. There is no night phase: your screen always shows your role and everything your role lets you know.
- The first captain is random. The captaincy passes clockwise after every proposal.

## Special roles (the host chooses which are in play)
| Role | Side | What they know |
|---|---|---|
| Navigator | crew | Sees every saboteur except the Ghost. Must stay hidden from the Hunter. |
| Hunter | saboteur | Always in play with the Navigator. Gets the final guess (see below). |
| Medic | crew | Sees who the Navigator is, but the Mimic looks identical. |
| Mimic | saboteur | Looks like the Navigator to the Medic. |
| Ghost | saboteur | Hidden from the Navigator. |
| Rogue | saboteur | Doesn't know the other saboteurs, and they don't know the Rogue. The Navigator does see the Rogue. |

Plain saboteurs (and the Hunter, Mimic and Ghost) know each other, except the Rogue. Plain crew know nothing.

## A round
1. **Propose:** the captain picks a crew of the required size for the current mission. They may include themselves.
2. **Vote:** everyone votes **approve** or **reject** at once. Votes are revealed together. A strict majority approves; a tie rejects.
   - Rejected: the next player becomes captain and proposes. **If 5 proposals in a row are rejected for one mission, the saboteurs win.**
3. **Mission:** each crew member secretly plays **success** or **sabotage**. Loyal crew can only play success. Only the number of sabotage cards is revealed. One sabotage fails the mission, except mission 4 with 7 or more players, which needs **two**.

Crew sizes per mission:

| Players | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| 6 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 4 |
| 7 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 4* | 4 |
| 8–10 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5* | 5 |

\* needs two sabotage cards to fail.

## Winning
- **Saboteurs** win when 3 missions fail, or when 5 proposals in a row are rejected.
- **Crew** wins when 3 missions succeed, unless the Navigator is in play. Then the Hunter reveals themselves and names one player as the Navigator. If the Hunter is right, the saboteurs win instead.

## In this app
- Click a player's name to see their voting history: every crew they voted on, how they voted, and what happened.
- A player leaving mid-game abandons that game (no winner), since a hidden-team game can't continue a seat short.
- The scoreboard gives a win to everyone on the winning side.
